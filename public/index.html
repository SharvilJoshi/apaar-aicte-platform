Capabilities

Beyond data collection — intelligent, monitored, verifiable.

01
⬆

Secure Data Submission API

Token-authenticated REST endpoints for single and bulk student records, scoped by institution API identity and role.

02
⛨

APAAR Validation Engine

12-digit format, Luhn check-digit, admission-year coherence and national duplicate resolution in under 10 ms for 10,000+ records.

03
⚠

Anomaly Detection

11 rules: intra-institution duplicates, cross-institution enrolment, invalid APAAR, intake overflow vs the year's sanctioned seats, quota-cap breaches, inconsistent NRI/OCI/FN data, missing student consent.

04
▤

Real-time Dashboards

KPI cards, intake-utilisation gauges, category & seat-type breakdowns, state-wise analytics and a live anomaly feed.

05
🔍

Stakeholder Verification

Employers and regulators verify any APAAR ID; role-scoped PII, hashed receipts, full verification history.

06
⬢

Verifiable Credentials

W3C-style JSON-LD credential per student, HMAC-SHA256 signed by the issuer — copyable into any wallet, independently verifiable.

07
⇄

Integration Ready

Plain JSON REST API with per-institution keys — integrates with institutional ERP, AICTE MIS, DigiLocker-style delivery.

08
⟲

Governance & Audit

RBAC (4 roles), immutable audit trail of logins/uploads/verifications/exports, student consent capture & revocation.

Role-based access

Four audiences. One repository. Least privilege everywhere.

ROLE 01

AICTE Central Admin

National oversight of every approved institution.

  • All institutions & records
  • Anomaly feed & intake breaches
  • Policy reports & CSV exports
  • Full audit trail
ROLE 02

Institution User

Data cell / registrar of one approved institution.

  • Upload & update own students
  • Programme intake vs enrolment
  • Consent management
  • Scoped audit log
ROLE 03

Authorized Verifier

Employers, regulators, awarding bodies.

  • Verify any APAAR ID
  • Academic fields only (PII masked)
  • Signature-validity proof
  • Verification receipt logged
ROLE 04

Public (unauthenticated)

Format validation without data exposure.

  • APAAR format & checksum check
  • No PII ever returned
  • Hint to approach authorized roles
  • Rate-safe read-only
